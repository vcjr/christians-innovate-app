## Problem Statement

77 members are signed up. 20-30 show up every Thursday for a recurring community call
(alternating between Bible reading recaps and builder demos). Between calls, the only
mid-week hook is the daily reading plan. Accountability groups exist but are a ghost
town. The platform feels like it's competing with YouVersion on Bible reading — a fight
it can't win.

The real question: how do we make the platform so essential to THIS community's identity
— Christian builders and innovators — that leaving it means losing connection to 70+
people who get the unique tension of building things while living out their faith?

## What Makes This Cool

Nobody is building this. YouVersion has 500M users and zero builder context. Slack and
Discord have builder communities with zero spiritual foundation. LinkedIn has professional
identity with no accountability depth. C12 is the closest analog (Christian executive
peer groups) but it's expensive, offline-only, and leadership-tier only.

This platform sits at the intersection: Bible-grounded community + builder accountability
+ shared weekly ritual (the Thursday call). That combination is defensible because it
requires all three to work, and no existing platform has all three.

## Constraints

- Small team (likely solo or very small)
- 77 members is the current base — any feature must work at this scale, not just at 10x
- Thursday call is NOT in the app — it's external (Zoom/Meet). The app must serve the
  call lifecycle, not host it.
- Existing features: reading plans, accountability groups, directory, comments, member
  profiles, email system (Resend)
- Stack: Next.js, Supabase, TypeScript, Tailwind — standard modern web

## Premises

1. The Thursday call is the core retention engine — the app should serve it, not replace it.
2. Competing with YouVersion on Bible reading is a losing battle. The differentiation is
   the builder identity.
3. Accountability groups are underused because there's no clear prompt for WHAT to be
   accountable about. Reading streaks alone aren't sticky enough for this audience.
4. Power users will be defined by builder activity (shipping, demos) + spiritual
   consistency — not reading streaks alone.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: "Builder Check-In" (Minimal Viable)
Weekly builder prompt inside accountability groups — 3 questions before each Thursday
call: "What are you building? What did you commit to last week? What do you need prayer
for this week?" Ships in days. Gives groups a clear structure and reason to engage.

- Effort: S
- Risk: Low
- Pros: Directly solves the "what do we do in a group?" problem. Builds on Thursday
  ritual. Immediately purposeful.
- Cons: Doesn't create standalone mid-week pull. No community-wide visibility.

### Approach B: "Builder Profile Layer" (Full Feature)
Builder identity layer on member profiles — current project, weekly shipping goals,
prayer requests tied to their work. Groups get a dashboard. Community feed shows
launches and wins. Dual power-user metric: reading streak + builder streak.

- Effort: L
- Risk: Med
- Pros: Makes platform identity visible as builder-first. Creates mid-week update
  motivation. Community feed serves the demo cadence.
- Cons: More surface area. Requires member buy-in on filling out profiles.

### Approach C: "Thursday-First OS" (Architectural North Star)
Redesign the app's weekly rhythm around the Thursday call lifecycle:
- Mon–Wed: reading + "what's your update for Thursday?" prompt
- Thu: call happens outside the app
- Post-call: log commitments, prayer requests, accountability check-ins auto-created
The app becomes the operating system of the Thursday meeting.

- Effort: XL
- Risk: High
- Pros: Thursday call drives app usage naturally. Every feature has a clear "when."
  Accountability groups get structure baked in.
- Cons: Bigger scope. Requires members to change how they think about the app.

## Recommended Approach

**A + C together: ship A as the wedge, design toward C as the north star.**

**This week (Approach A):**
Add a recurring weekly prompt inside accountability groups. Three questions, auto-sent
Monday morning via the existing email system (Resend):
1. "What are you building or working on this week?"
2. "What did you commit to last week — did you do it?"
3. "What do you need prayer for this week?"

Responses log to the group feed. On Thursday, the accountability group has a live
summary of what everyone's working on — that's the pre-call briefing.

**The north star (Approach C):**
The weekly rhythm of the platform is: Mon prompt → Tue/Wed reading + response →
Thu call → Thu/Fri post-call commitments logged → repeat. Every feature in the
app earns its place by serving one phase of that cycle.

**What makes someone a power user in this model:**
- Active in an accountability group (sending responses, engaging with others')
- Completing daily reading
- Showing up at Thursday calls
- Logging a project or build

This dual identity — builder + Scripture reader — is the unlock. YouVersion users
have reading streaks. Power users here have accountability streaks.

## Open Questions

1. **Group formation**: Members can browse open groups via the directory and click
   "Request to Join" — group admin approves. For bootstrap: admin manually places the
   first 10–15 most engaged members into 2–3 groups to seed activity before self-serve
   is fully trusted. Auto-suggestions (based on project domain or timezone) are a later
   iteration.
2. **Thursday call integration**: The "Thursday prep view" is accessed via a button on
   the accountability group page. It shows only responses from that group's members to
   this week's prompt. Available from Thursday 6am until end of day. Read-only, no new
   input.
3. **Accountability streak definition**: A streak is earned by responding to the weekly
   builder prompt in any given week (Mon–Sun). Missing one full week breaks the streak
   and resets to 0. Consecutive weeks = streak count.
4. **Visibility**: Builder check-ins are private to the group by default. Community-wide
   visibility is an opt-in the member controls per-response ("share with all members").
5. **Demo tracking**: Deferred — Thursday demo submission flow is a future feature once
   group engagement is proven.

## Success Criteria

**30 days:**
- 3+ accountability groups actively using the weekly builder prompt
- 50%+ of group members responding to at least one prompt per week
- Accountability group feature mentioned organically on a Thursday call

**90 days:**
- Power users (dual activity: reading + builder check-in) identifiable in the data
- Mid-week app engagement measurably up from baseline
- At least one "I shipped X because of accountability group pressure" story from a member

**The leading indicator to watch:** Thursday call attendance vs. accountability group
response rate. If they track together, the theory is right. If they diverge, something's off.

## Distribution Plan

This is a web app deployed on Vercel — existing pipeline covers new features. No new
distribution needed.

**Email delivery (weekly prompt via Resend):**
- Sent to active group members Monday at 9am ET
- Members can opt out per-group via app settings
- Failed sends retried at 6pm ET same day and 6pm following day
- No throttling needed at 77-member scale

**Power user data contract:**
- Every check-in response is timestamped and linked to user + group in Supabase
- Power user definition: 4+ week reading streak AND 3+ check-in responses in the
  last 4 weeks
- Queryable via Supabase; surfaced in admin dashboard by Week 8 of implementation
